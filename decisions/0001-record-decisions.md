# 0001. Record design decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13

## Context

The framework is developed by a distributed group of human and AI contributors over a long period.
The reasons behind design choices are easily lost in meetings, slides and chat, and are then re-argued.

## Decision

Record significant design decisions as short, numbered Markdown files in `decisions/`, using `template.md`.
The framework text links to the decision records it depends on.

## Consequences

Contributors, including AI agents, can find out why something is the way it is before proposing to change it.
Decisions are reviewed through pull requests, like the text.
